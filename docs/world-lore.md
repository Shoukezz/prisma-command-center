# World Lore

Year: 2035

After the Global Resource Crisis, the world split into three major blocs.

## Aster Union

Technology-driven alliance.

Strengths:
- cyber warfare
- intelligence
- satellites

## Nordex Federation

Military superpower.

Strengths:
- conventional forces
- industrial capacity

## Eastern Consortium

Economic powerhouse.

Strengths:
- logistics
- manufacturing
- influence operations

The world is unstable.

Regional conflicts constantly emerge.

The player works for PRISMA.

PRISMA is an international strategic operations center.
